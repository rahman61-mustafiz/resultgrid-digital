### E11 proposed file naming and organization

```text
clients/{client-slug}/projects/{project-slug}/{category}/
YYYY-MM-DD_{document-type}_{short-subject}_vNN.ext
```

Rules:

- Store stable workspace/client/project/document IDs in database metadata; a filename is not the relationship key.
- Use ISO date `YYYY-MM-DD`, lowercase slugs, and two-digit versions such as `v01`.
- Categories: `brand`, `evidence`, `reports`, `financial`, `creative`, `seo`, `ads`, `website`, `contracts`, `exports`.

### E11 proposed freshness rule

- Weekly operational metrics: expected update every 7 days; after 7 days show `STALE` with last-updated date.

- Unavailable data is never displayed as zero.
